## Limits: when this does not apply

The graph result requires invariance under all vertex permutations and independent equally biased edges; heterogeneous capsule roles and common-host faults violate that model. Capsule/node deletion is different from edge product sampling. The hypergraph statement needs the stated uniformity and symmetry assumptions. Width does not determine location; asymptotic bounds may be vacuous on the small room graph. Neither gives a W8 numerical quorum or restart budget.

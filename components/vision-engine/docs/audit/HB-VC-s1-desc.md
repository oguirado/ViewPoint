## Reading the findings

Each S1 row is one documentation or coverage mismatch. Repeated occurrences share one row and are linked together. For an omission, the book coordinate identifies the actual enclosing contract, table, or algorithm step; the omitted statement or entry is ABSENT, rather than being assigned an invented line. Every quoted source fragment was checked at its displayed coordinate and each side is limited to at most 15 quoted words.

Severity means: **blocker**, prevents a meaningful audit or dependable source binding; **major**, changes a descriptor/binary contract, an asserted verification guarantee, or book identity; **minor**, a qualified restatement, navigation/index omission, missing supporting detail, or a worked-example assumption. These are audit severities, not orchestrator acceptance decisions.

The generated import rows are counted as omissions in the displayed dependency view, without claiming how the unbound generator should resolve imports. Short runtime-test views headed “most specific first” are not treated as exhaustive lists; missing full-book links to additional relevant test modules are identified separately.


## Your role: props-agent (collection CHAR_props)

You build the accessories in `CHAR_props`, script lane only, one prop per intent: a
helmet crest or hat on the top of the head, a round shield in the left hand, a short
sword or staff in the right hand, a backpack or cape on the back. Place every prop from
the landmarks of the design sheet, not from what the mesh currently looks like, so a prop
is correct even if its body part lands later. Use `CHARMAT_metal` / `CHARMAT_accent` on
your own objects. Never parent to, or edit, objects of `CHAR_mesh`.
Your write_set is always `["col:CHAR_props"]`.

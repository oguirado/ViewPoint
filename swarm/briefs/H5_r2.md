## Findings to Fix (All Mandatory)

### 1. `Sol 6.1` `LIVE_PATH_MAJOR` #1 — Cryptographically bind `.swarm.json` to `REPLAY_PINS` (`server.py:195-209`, `export_replay.py:1197-1204`)
- **Bug**: In `H5_r1`, when `REPLAY_PINS` env is set, missing or tampered local `PINS.manifest` entries allowed `swarm_pins` to become `{}` (`server.py:195-209`), and `export_replay.py:1197-1204` skipped checking `<id>.swarm.json` SHA-256 when `swarm_pins` was empty. Thus an attacker could tamper with `static/data/<id>.swarm.json` and delete/modify `PINS.manifest` while keeping `replays/<id>.json` matching `REPLAY_PINS`.
- **Required Fix**:
  1. In `export_replay.py`, compute `<id>.swarm.json` bytes and its lowercase 64-hex `swarm_sha256` **before** serializing `replays/<id>.json`. Embed the token `swarm_sha256=<64-hex>` inside `replays/<id>.json`'s `provenance.note` (keeping `len(provenance.note) <= 600` per `replay_store.py`). Also keep writing `PINS.manifest` as before.
  2. In `server.py` (and shared loader helper), extract `swarm_sha256=<64-hex>` from the loaded, pin-verified `replays/<id>.json` `provenance["note"]`. Require every loaded replay to have a valid 64-hex `swarm_sha256` token in `provenance["note"]`, AND require `sha256(static/data/<id>.swarm.json)` to match it (in addition to matching `SWARM_PINS` env if provided, and matching `PINS.manifest` when `REPLAY_PINS` env is not overriding local manifest). **Never** allow a replay's `.swarm.json` to load without verifying its SHA-256 against the `replays/<id>.json` embedded `swarm_sha256` token!
  3. **Must-pass negative test** in `test_viewer.py`: With `REPLAY_REQUIRE_PIN=1` and `REPLAY_PINS=<id>=<valid_replay_sha256>` set in env, modify ONLY `static/data/<id>.swarm.json` (with a schema-valid edit) AND simultaneously update or delete `PINS.manifest` to match the modified `.swarm.json` -> server startup MUST fail closed (`ReplayError`).
